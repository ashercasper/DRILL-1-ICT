    2. Adrianne Lenker

    - listen on Spotify here


    Adrianne Lenker is a solo artist who also served as a member and main vocalist of the band Big Thief. Her songs, using flowy guitar and emotion-packed vocals, explore themes of love, loss, self-discovery and the like. She is known for her album songs, which contains anything, forward beckon rebound, not a lot, just forever, and others. I highly recommend listening to her, because her songs feel like a much-needed warm embrace from a friend.

    3. The Cure

    - listen on Spotify here


    The Cure is a British rock band formed in 1976 in London. They are known for their distinctive sound, which blends post-punk and gothic rock elements. Their music often features dark, atmospheric themes and innovative use of synthesizers and drum machines. Some of their most popular songs include "Just Like Heaven", "Friday I'm in Love", "The Lovecats", and "Boys Don't Cry". I listen to them frequently because their songs are quick, easy to learn on guitar, and resonate deeply with what I am feeling.

    fun fact: "Just Like Heaven" and "Boys Don't Cry" have spent 5 weeks as my most-listened-to song during the summer.
